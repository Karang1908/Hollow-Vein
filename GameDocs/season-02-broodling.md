## PUZZLE DETAIL: Tunnel Maze (dual-path)
- **Primary path:** Analyst reads sonar pings (recon drone), calls turns to Infiltrator in real time — fastest, quietest.
- **Fallback path:** if Analyst's drone is destroyed/unavailable, the team can brute-force navigate via visible bio-residue trails on walls — slower (~2x time), noisier, but never blocks progress.
- Struggle Assist: after 3 wrong turns taken, a faint light/sound cue marks the correct route.

---

## ENCOUNTER DETAIL
Standard tier: Thrall packs of 3–5 throughout the maze (Idle→Alert states, easily avoided via crouch/prone), first Crawlers appear singly as ambush enemies from side-vents (2–3 total before the boss). Barricade Hold phase: a sustained wave of 6–8 Thralls testing Breacher's solo tanking — Adaptive Scaling reduces wave size after 2 failed holds.

---

## BOSS DETAIL: The Broodling (1:35–2:20, ~45 min)
- **Phase 1 (0–40% boss HP):** fast melee-focused, low telegraph windows. Breacher tanks/blocks, Infiltrator flanks for bonus damage windows, Analyst calls attack tells 0.5s early via sound cue.
- **Phase 2 (40–15%):** gas-hazard phase begins — Sentinel manages a shared oxygen meter while the fight continues; boss retreats periodically to regen unless pressured.
- **Phase 3 (15–0%):** enrage — faster attacks, but a clear, telegraphed final opening. Boss Mercy Toggle available after 3 full wipes on this fight specifically.
- No unsolvable state: all four roles have a designated job through every phase; a downed teammate shifts their job to whichever role is free (Breacher can hold aggro solo if Infiltrator is down, etc.), just at reduced efficiency.
