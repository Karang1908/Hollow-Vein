## MECHANIC DETAIL: Light-Sharing (Blackout Approach)
Only one light source exists for the whole squad. It can be picked up, carried, or deliberately dropped/passed between players. Carrying it slows movement slightly and marks the carrier to enemies; not carrying it means fighting blind. Role rotates naturally under pressure — no fixed assignment, which is the point: it's meant to force real-time squad communication rather than a scripted swap.

---

## BOSS DETAIL: The Broodmother (1:00–2:00, ~1 hr)
- **Phase 1 (0–50% HP):** weak points only visible when directly lit — Analyst calls them out the instant light hits, Breacher tanks charge attacks, Infiltrator flanks during recovery windows.
- **Phase 2 (50–20%):** poison-spore field phase — Sentinel manages team health/exposure while the fight continues, light-bearer role becomes even more critical as visibility narrows further.
- **Phase 3 (20–0%):** the Broodmother summons Crawler/Burster adds during a brief recovery window — team must choose to burn the adds or push the boss, a real tactical decision each time it triggers.
- Boss Mercy Toggle available after 3 full wipes. Dual-path guarantee: if the light-bearer goes down, any other player can pick up the light immediately — never a hard stop.
