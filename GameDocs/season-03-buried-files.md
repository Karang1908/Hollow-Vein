## PUZZLE DETAIL: Parallel Terminal Hack (dual-path)
- **Primary path:** Infiltrator hacks each terminal (~2 min each), Analyst runs a secondary decryption sub-task simultaneously to shave time off the shared lockdown timer.
- **Fallback path:** if Infiltrator is down, Analyst can hack alone at reduced speed (no time-shave bonus) — never blocks the mission, just removes the speed bonus.
- Struggle Assist: after 3 failed hack stages on any terminal, the correct next input is highlighted.
- The **shared lockdown timer** starts the moment *any* terminal is touched — a genuine coordination test between the two pairs, but capped so it never expires before at least 2 of 3 terminals are reachable even at a slow pace (guarantees the mission is completable, just harder to do fully clean).
