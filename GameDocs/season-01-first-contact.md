## THE THREE APPROACH PATHS (0:05–0:20)

- **Break In** — Breacher forces a side door (tutorial: breaching mechanic); Infiltrator disables one camera loop; Sentinel/Analyst hold the perimeter, calling out patrol timing.
- **Inside Job** — Infiltrator badges the team in via a planted staff ID; Analyst watches camera feeds for patrol gaps; Breacher/Sentinel wait on the signal to move — teaches patience/timing over force.
- **Standoff** — a rival crew already has hostages inside; Sentinel talks down tension (dialogue-choice mini-system) while Breacher/Infiltrator flank — teaches the dialogue-choice UI early.

All three paths are timed to reconverge at 0:20 regardless of route, so squad pacing stays consistent for the vault phase.

---

## PUZZLE DETAIL: Vault Access (dual-path)
- **Path A (Infiltrator hack):** 3-stage terminal hack minigame, ~90 seconds if done cleanly. Struggle Assist triggers after 3 failed stage attempts, highlighting the correct next input.
- **Path B (Breacher force):** manual breach sequence — timed strength-meter mini-game, louder (draws 1 extra guard patrol), but always succeeds given enough attempts, no fail-state.
- Either path is valid; mixing (Infiltrator starts, Breacher finishes if Infiltrator goes down) is explicitly supported.

---

## ENCOUNTER DETAIL
Standard tier: 2–4 bank guards per approach path, no armor, low aggression (Idle→Suspicious states dominate; true Combat state rare unless stealth fails). Standoff path adds a 3-person rival crew as the only fully-human "boss-adjacent" fight in Season 1 — deliberately, so the first Thrall sighting during extraction lands as a tonal shift.

---

## THE CRACK (0:35–0:45)
Scripted structurally, but **player-caused**: whoever forced/hacked the vault triggers a QTE-style prompt during the final action. Whichever NPC (teammate is never permanently harmed — always an NPC: a guard, a hostage, or a rival crew member) is nearest when the prompt resolves gets the hairline black-gold crack. This NPC identity is logged and carried forward as the target of Season 2.

---

## BRANCH POINTS
- Approach path (Break In / Inside Job / Standoff) — cosmetic/tonal only in Season 1, no mechanical difficulty difference by design (this season teaches, doesn't test).
- Vault path (hack vs. force) — affects noise level and which NPC is likely nearest the crack, which affects Season 2 flavor text only.
