## PUZZLE DETAIL: Sentry Drone Hacking (dual-path)
- **Primary path:** Infiltrator remote-hacks drones from cover (Analyst marks them first for a hack-speed bonus).
- **Fallback path:** any player can EMP a drone directly (short-range throwable) — slower to clear a full patrol, louder, but always works even solo.

---

## ENCOUNTER DETAIL
Convoy Squad: 2–3 escort vehicles, Dominion Enforcers on foot at the hijack point. Sample-Site Squad: 3–4 Sentry Drones on patrol, plus a timed reinforcement wave if the countdown isn't beaten. Resolution Combat density scales with the convergence choice (Sabotage = highest density, Ally = lowest, reflecting Esparza providing brief cover fire).

---

## BRANCH POINTS: Convergence Choice (1:00–1:20)
- **Steal:** balanced difficulty, full Dominion research recovered, no lasting alliance.
- **Sabotage:** hardest combat resolution, denies Dominion the tech, small reputation loss with Esparza (affects her S10/S11 recruitability).
- **Ally with Esparza:** easiest combat resolution (brief NPC support fire), partial tech access only, sets up her potential recruitment in Season 10.
