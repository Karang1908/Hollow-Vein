## MECHANIC DETAIL: No-Auto-Respawn Revive
From this season on, a downed teammate does **not** automatically return at a checkpoint — they must be physically reached and revived by Sentinel (or, at a time penalty, any player who's picked up basic revive charges from Season 10's medic sub-crafting). This raises real stakes without ever fully removing a downed player from the mission permanently — there's always a path back.

---

## MECHANIC DETAIL: Hangar Sequence (dual-path)
- **Primary path:** Infiltrator bypasses hangar security quietly (~2 min), Analyst preps the aircraft, Breacher/Sentinel hold the tarmac against a light Dominion/Vein incursion.
- **Fallback path:** if the quiet bypass fails 3+ times (Struggle Assist triggers a hint), Breacher can force the hangar door instead — louder, triggers a full incursion wave immediately, but always works.

---

## ENCOUNTER DETAIL
Wave Defense: full roster wave composition (Thralls, Crawlers, Bursters, Sentry Drones, Cipher Sentinels), density scaled down in lanes that were fortified in Season 10 and scaled up in lanes that weren't — a direct, transparent consequence system rather than hidden difficulty. Airfield/Hangar: light Dominion resistance. Aerial Intercept: minor aerial hazards (debris, defensive fire from the scout) rather than full dogfighting — keeps the flight mechanic approachable on its first real use.

---

## BOSS DETAIL: The Scout-Construct (2:10–2:30)
- Single-phase, intentionally shorter and more forgiving than prior boss fights — this is a **tutorial** for the Starbreaker's co-op channel mechanic, not the hardest fight in the season.
- All four players must channel into the half-built Starbreaker simultaneously for a brief window to land the finishing blow — first time this exact "all four, same action" beat occurs, deliberately under-tuned here so players learn it before Season 12 demands it under real pressure.
