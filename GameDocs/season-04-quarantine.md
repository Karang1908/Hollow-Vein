## BRANCH DETAIL: Quiet Evac vs. Public Exposure
- **Quiet Evac:** stealth-focused, avoid cult patrols, slower but lower combat density — rewards Infiltrator-heavy play.
- **Public Exposure:** loud, faster civilian movement (broadcast alert speeds evacuation) but higher combat density and Fanatic aggression — rewards Breacher-heavy play.
- Both paths are fully completable at Standard difficulty; Public Exposure earns more district-trust reputation (affects later-season NPC dialogue/flavor), Quiet Evac earns a small stealth-bonus point multiplier.
- Dual-path guarantee: any locked apartment door blocking a civilian can be hacked (Infiltrator) or forced (Breacher) — no single-role dependency.
