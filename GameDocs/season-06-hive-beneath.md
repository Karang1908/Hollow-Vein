## PUZZLE DETAIL: Sonar Navigation (dual-path)
- **Primary path:** Analyst reads live sonar, whisper-calls turns — fastest and quietest.
- **Fallback path:** if Analyst is downed, the team can navigate via visible residue trails at a much slower pace and higher noise-meter risk — never blocks progress, meaningfully raises difficulty.
- Struggle Assist: after 3 wrong turns, faint directional audio cue plays.
- **Noise meter:** fills on sprinting, gunfire, or loud melee; at full, triggers a swarm response. Meter drains automatically over time when the team is quiet — never a hard fail-state, just an escalating pressure system.
