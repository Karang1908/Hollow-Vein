## FORTIFICATION SUB-MISSIONS (choose 2 of 3, ~35 min each)
- **Downtown reinforcement:** defend a barricade-building sequence against a timed Cipher Sentinel/Thrall wave.
- **Lowfen reinforcement:** escort a supply convoy through cult-contested streets (vehicle + combat hybrid).
- **Docks reinforcement:** clear a Hive Construct nest threatening the harbor's defensive perimeter.

Each is fully self-contained and completable solo-difficulty-scaled if the squad chooses to split 2+2 to cover two at once — no requirement to do all three, which keeps the season from over-running its target runtime while still giving real strategic choice weight for Season 11.

---

## MECHANIC DETAIL: Forge Tutorial
Players deposit their accumulated Vein Shards into the Ember Vault forge for the first time — a shared crafting-sequence minigame requiring all four to contribute simultaneously (each role interacts with a different forge subsystem: Breacher stokes the core, Infiltrator stabilizes the containment field, Sentinel regulates output, Analyst calibrates the resonance). Talia narrates the shortfall at the end, motivating the hard-mode replay loop honestly rather than as an arbitrary grind wall.

## ENCOUNTER DETAIL
Cipher Sentinels and Thrall/Crawler defense waves scaled to whichever fortification sub-missions are chosen — Standard tier density, Adaptive Scaling applies per sub-mission independently.
