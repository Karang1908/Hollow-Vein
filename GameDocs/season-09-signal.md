## PUZZLE DETAIL: Cipher Sentinels (dual-path)
Cipher Sentinels are puzzle-combat hybrids — they only take damage while a **specific role performs a specific action** simultaneously (e.g., only vulnerable while Analyst has them marked AND Breacher is in melee range). This previews Kryptex's own fight logic in Season 12.
- **Primary path:** correctly identify and execute the role-combo — fast, clean kills.
- **Fallback path:** sustained generic damage from all four still kills them eventually, just far slower and riskier — never a hard requirement to "solve" the combo correctly.
- Struggle Assist: after 3 failed attempts at a given Sentinel, its vulnerability window is highlighted visually.

---

## MECHANIC DETAIL: Safe Choir-Tech Interface (Transmitter Room)
Only Infiltrator can safely interface with Choir technology without triggering a defensive response — established here as a specialization that pays off again in Season 12. If Infiltrator is down, another player can attempt it at a flat time penalty and a guaranteed defensive-wave trigger (still completable, just harder).
