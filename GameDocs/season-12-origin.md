## MECHANIC DETAIL: Phase 4 — The Channel (2:20–2:50)
The Starbreaker only fires if all four players are channeling into it **at the same time**, sustaining the channel through a brief vulnerability window Kryptex opens roughly every 90 seconds. Breaking the channel (any player interrupted by taking a hit) resets the charge but does **not** reset the whole phase — a Struggle Assist–equivalent system here reduces the required channel duration slightly after 2 failed attempts, ensuring this final, most narratively important beat is never the point where a squad gets permanently stuck after 3+ hours of play.

---

## ENCOUNTER DETAIL
Phases 1–3 are supported by Kryptex's own Cipher Sentinel constructs (previewed since Season 9) rather than the general bestiary — reinforcing that this final fight is a distilled, harder version of mechanics the team has already learned, not a pile of brand-new content. Boss Mercy Toggle available after 3 full wipes on Phase 4 specifically, given its length and stakes.
