## SITE DETAIL: Four Solo Encounters (0:10–1:30)
- **Breacher's site:** Hive Construct nest defending a rooftop altar — heavy solo combat, tests tanking/positioning without backup.
- **Infiltrator's site:** rooftop sniper nest requiring stealth traversal via grapple hook — tests solo stealth movement.
- **Sentinel's site:** street-level Thrall swarm requiring careful ammo/resource conservation — tests solo resource management (no teammate to revive them here, so downs are more costly).
- **Analyst's site:** Brother Calder's personal guard, a scripted near-boss-tier fight — the hardest single site, deliberately positioned so it's the one most likely to need cross-site support.

Each site has its own dual-path solution appropriate to its role (e.g., Infiltrator's site can be brute-forced loudly if the stealth route fails 3+ times, triggering Struggle Assist).

---

## MECHANIC DETAIL: Cross-Site Remote Support (1:30–1:50)
A player who finishes their site early can call in a single remote support action for a teammate still struggling — a marked airstrike-style ping, a supply drop, or a distraction, role-flavored (e.g., Analyst calls a marking-flare, Breacher can't physically help but can radio a positioning callout). This exists specifically so the hardest site (Analyst's, vs. Calder's guard) isn't a hard-wall for a struggling player.

---

## ENCOUNTER DETAIL
Full-strength Awakened Fanatics across all sites (packs with a chanting "anchor" enemy — kill it first to break pack buffs, consistent with Season 4's introduction of the mechanic), a Hive Construct nest (Breacher's site), rooftop snipers (Infiltrator's site), Calder's personal guard (Analyst's site, near-boss tier).

---

## BRANCH POINTS: Countdown Resolution (1:50–2:10)
- **All 4 sites cleared before the countdown:** ritual fully disrupted, best outcome, sets an easier Season 9 opening state.
- **1–2 sites incomplete:** ritual partially succeeds, harder Season 9 opening state, but never a season failure — the mission still resolves and progresses.

## FRAGMENT/LOOT PLACEMENT
